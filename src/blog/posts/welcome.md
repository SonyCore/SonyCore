# Welcome 👋

This is the first post on my new blog. I've spent the last few years writing
backend services in **Golang** and automating things in **Python**, and I
figured it was about time to write some of those notes down.

## What you'll find here

- Field notes from building backend systems — the kinds of problems that
  don't fit a tutorial.
- Short pieces on **DevOps**: containers, CI/CD pipelines, Linux
  internals, and the occasional networking deep-dive.
- Open-source updates from projects like
  [`namira-core`](https://github.com/SonyCore/namira-core) and
  [`XRayGen`](https://github.com/SonyCore/XRayGen).
